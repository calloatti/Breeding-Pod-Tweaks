Breeding pods continue running when paused by automation until reaching 90-99% progress, so that when they are enabled by automation they are almost finished.

When automation pauses a breeding pod, the pod continues running if growth progress is below the random 90-99% target. A custom yellow border "Paused by automation" status icon displays instead of the vanilla "Paused by automation" icon. Once progress reaches the target, the pod allows the automation pause and shows the standard paused icon.

Manual pauses (player clicking pause button) are unaffected and show the vanilla pause icon. If a building is both manually paused and automation tries to pause, the manual pause takes precedence and no custom icon displays.

Works with both regular Breeding Pods and Advanced Breeding Pods (Iron Teeth).
