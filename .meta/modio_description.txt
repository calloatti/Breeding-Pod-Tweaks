<p>Breeding pods continue running when paused by automation until reaching 90-99% progress, so that when they are enabled by automation they are almost finished.</p>
<p>When automation pauses a breeding pod, the pod continues running if growth progress is below the random 90-99% target. A custom yellow border &quot;Paused by automation&quot; status icon displays instead of the vanilla &quot;Paused by automation&quot; icon. Once progress reaches the target, the pod allows the automation pause and shows the standard paused icon.</p>
<p>Manual pauses (player clicking pause button) are unaffected and show the vanilla pause icon. If a building is both manually paused and automation tries to pause, the manual pause takes precedence and no custom icon displays.</p>
<p>Works with both regular Breeding Pods and Advanced Breeding Pods (Iron Teeth).</p>
